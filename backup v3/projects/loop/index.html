Project documentation

Design rationale & next steps

Problem

Points are often presented as abstract balances rather than understandable, useful choices at checkout.

Design hypothesis

Showing the reward value, remaining points, and checkout impact together may reduce uncertainty.

Validation plan

Observe whether shoppers can compare rewards, predict the total, and recover from an unavailable option.
