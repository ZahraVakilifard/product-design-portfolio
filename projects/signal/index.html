Reading the evidence

Signals are not causes

Changes in engagement may indicate where to investigate. This prototype deliberately separates observations from hypotheses and avoids claiming that a suggested action will improve retention.

Project documentation

Design rationale & next steps

Problem

Merchants can see aggregate metrics but struggle to identify where customers disengage and what evidence supports a decision.

Design hypothesis

A cohort-first journey explorer can make investigation more approachable without presenting correlation as causation.

Validation plan

Test cohort comparison, stage comprehension, interpretation errors, and the ability to distinguish evidence from hypotheses.
