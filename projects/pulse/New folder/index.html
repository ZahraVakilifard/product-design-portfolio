THE CASE STUDY / SELF-INITIATED CONCEPT

Build a campaign system that adapts without losing its voice.

Below is the design rationale for the working prototype above. This is a proposed solution, not a commissioned project or a validated user-research outcome.

AUDIENCE

Small brand teams preparing multiple campaign variants across social and on-site placements.

DESIGN GOAL

Choose an audience, adjust messaging, change a visual direction, and preview square or vertical creative.

CONSTRAINTS

Different aspect ratios, text length, contrast, brand recognition, and consistent offer hierarchy.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

What needs to change?

Marketing assets often drift apart as audiences, offers, and formats multiply. The challenge is to keep a recognizable brand expression while allowing purposeful variation.

02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Rules for the experience.

01

System over one-off

Use a small set of expressive rules rather than unrelated posters.

02

Message first

Preserve the offer and call-to-action hierarchy across variants.

03

Make variation intentional

Expose audience, tone, color, and format as explicit decisions.

03 / INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

One connected journey.

  1. 01Select audience
  2. 02Set voice
  3. 03Choose color
  4. 04Adjust offer
  5. 05Preview format

Conceptual task flow, represented by the interactive prototype above. No participant testing has been conducted.

04 / DESIGN EXPLORATION

Choices and trade-offs.

DECISION 01

Audience-specific copy

Copy variants give the system a purpose beyond cosmetic theme changes.

Trade-off: Risk: actual campaigns need copy review and brand governance.

DECISION 02

Limited palettes

Three controlled themes demonstrate range while maintaining a family resemblance.

Trade-off: Risk: some combinations may need more contrast testing.

DECISION 03

Format switch

A story layout demonstrates responsive art direction, not just scaling.

Trade-off: Risk: a production tool needs safe areas and text overflow handling.

05 / DESIGN ITERATION

From static templates to token-driven responsive art direction.

V1 / INITIAL WIREFRAME

Fixed-Ratio Static Layouts

Initial concepts required separate templates per social channel, causing offer callouts and contrast ratios to break when switching to vertical story formats.

V2 / FINAL SOLUTION

Tokenized Multi-Ratio Engine

Unified typography scale, paired contrast tokens, and layout ratios so any audience or tone variant maintains brand hierarchy across both 4:4 and 9:16 formats.

07 / STATES & EDGE CASES

Beyond the happy path.

  • New / returning / VIP audience
  • Bold / warm / minimal voice
  • Square / story format
  • Low / high offer value
08 / VALIDATION PLAN

What we would test next.

  • Can viewers recognize the same brand across formats?
  • Is the offer understandable within a quick glance?
  • Does copy remain readable on narrow screens?

Success signals to measure: Offer comprehension, perceived consistency, contrast and layout audit.

These are proposed evaluation criteria. No participant interviews, test results, conversion lift, or client impact are represented as completed work.
