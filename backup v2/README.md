# Zahra Vakilifard — Product Design Portfolio (2026)

A custom-designed and hand-coded product design portfolio showcasing four end-to-end conceptual product systems, interactive prototypes, design tokens, and validation plans.

## Projects

- **SIGNAL** (`projects/signal/`) — Cohort explorer for B2B merchant retention diagnostics; selectable cohorts, periods, journey stages, and testable hypotheses.
- **LOOP** (`projects/loop/`) — Consumer mobile reward redemption wallet with real-time balance math and checkout preview.
- **LAUNCHPAD** (`projects/launchpad/`) — Split-screen merchant loyalty onboarding studio with rule validation and live customer card preview.
- **PULSE** (`projects/pulse/`) — Token-driven multi-ratio campaign art direction and responsive creative engine.

## Structure

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js` — Main portfolio landing page, architectural layout, and canvas hero animation.
- `projects/shared.css` & `projects/case-study.css` — Shared design tokens and deep-dive case study styles.
- `projects/<project>/` — Individual interactive prototypes and case study pages.
